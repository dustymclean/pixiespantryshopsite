The Audit

The Audit

A transparent review framework for hardware quality, compatibility, airflow, materials, cleaning access, and use-case fit.

View Hardware Get Support

System blocks.

Vapor path material

We verify the structural integrity and thermal off-gassing profile of every material the aerosol touches. No mystery metals, no cheap silicones.

Thermal stability

We track how the hardware handles sustained heat. If it degrades, warps, or introduces risk under normal thermal load, it fails the audit.

Cleaning access

If you can't clean it, you can't use it repeatedly. We require full access to the airpath for standard chemical sterilization agents.

Joint compatibility

Tolerances matter. We verify standard millimeter sizing (10mm, 14mm, 18mm) to ensure parts seat correctly without air leaks.

Airflow behavior

We evaluate the draw resistance and choke points. A system needs predictable pressure regulation to function properly.

Modularity

Hardware should grow with your needs. We look for cross-compatibility and clear upgrade paths.

Packaging and documentation

Manufacturers must provide clear, English-language operating and safety instructions. Zero guesswork.

Vendor verification

We trace the supply chain. You get hardware sourced directly from the manufacturer or a verified authorized distributor.

Final operating principle.

Every page must answer what this hardware is, why Pixie’s Pantry carries it, how it fits into a system, how to clean it, what it is compatible with, or what to do next.
