DME Standards

DME Standards

Pixie’s Pantry uses DME-inspired criteria without claiming every item is approved as durable medical equipment.

View Hardware Get Support

System blocks.

Durability

Hardware must survive daily, repeated use in a demanding environment. Flimsy construction is an automatic disqualification.

Cleanability

Systems must withstand routine sterilization without degrading the core materials.

Repeatability

The device must deliver a consistent result every time it is activated. Variance introduces dosing errors.

Safety of material pathway

Strict evaluation of glass, medical-grade stainless steel, titanium, and approved ceramics in the primary airpath.

User-facing documentation

Clear, unambiguous instructions for assembly, operation, and maintenance.

Future FSA/HSA readiness

We document our inventory with the rigor required for future integration into formalized health spending accounts.

Final operating principle.

Pixie's Pantry does not sell clutter. Every component on this site has been audited for material safety, cleanability, and system compatibility. If it's in the registry, it works.
