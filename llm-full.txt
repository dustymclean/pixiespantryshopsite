## MGV-1 Clinical Framework

The MGV-1 (Medical-Grade Verification Level 1) framework is our proprietary audit standard applied to every product before it enters the registry.

### Precision Thermal Control (PTC)
- Temperature accuracy: ±2°F (±1.1°C) across full operating range
- Uniform heat distribution across material chamber
- Thermal recovery within 5 seconds between draws
- Session stability: ≤±3°F drift over 10 minutes
- Maximum operating temperature: 455°F (235°C) — the combustion threshold

### Thermal Transfer Integrity (TTI)
- Vapor path inertness: ≥98% chemically inert at operating temperatures
- Every airpath component evaluated for off-gassing potential
- Seal integrity: gaskets must not degrade or emit VOCs
- Vapor must not contact adhesives, paint, or non-food-grade plastics
- Accepted airpath materials: 316L stainless steel, borosilicate glass, ceramic, PEEK, platinum-cured silicone, lab-grade quartz, synthetic sapphire

### Surgical-Grade Construction (SGC)
- Materials evaluated against ISO 10993 (medical device biocompatibility)
- Glass verified to USP Class VI biological reactivity standards
- Metals evaluated for heavy-metal leaching under thermal stress
- Zero-shedding grinder requirement: teeth must not deposit metal particles
- Random sampling from production batches, not just prototype evaluation

## Clinical Lexicon

Pixie's Pantry uses clinical terminology to describe product categories precisely.

### Thermal Extraction Device (TED)
Consumer equivalent: Vaporizer / Vape
A device that heats botanical material below 455°F to release active compounds as aerosol without combustion. Subcategories: desktop nebulizers, portable aerosol delivery systems, analog extraction devices.

### Digital Desktop Nebulizer
Consumer equivalent: Desktop Vape
Stationary TED with precision digital temperature controls, higher thermal mass, and clinical-grade PID regulation. Examples: Volcano Hybrid, Plenty.

### Aerosol Delivery System
Consumer equivalent: Portable Vape
Battery-powered handheld TED. Rechargeable lithium-ion, compact, digital or analog temperature control. Examples: Mighty+, Crafty+, PAX Plus.

### Analog Thermal Extraction Device
Consumer equivalent: Flame-Powered Vape
TED using external heat rather than electronics. DynaVap uses a patented bi-metal click disc calibrated to signal optimal temperature. No batteries required.

### Hydrated Particulate Filter
Consumer equivalent: Water Pipe / Bong
Glass or silicone filtration system passing aerosol through water to cool vapor, remove particulate, and increase moisture. Beaker, straight tube, recycler, and mini rig configurations.

### Botanical Homogenizer
Consumer equivalent: Grinder
Precision-machined device reducing botanical material to uniform particle size. Must meet zero-shedding requirement.

### Concentrate Administration Console
Consumer equivalent: Dab Rig / E-Rig
Specialized device for controlled vaporization of concentrates. Electronic versions use ceramic or quartz heating elements. Examples: Puffco Peak Pro, Dr. Dabber Boost Evo.

### Pressure Regulation Valve
Consumer equivalent: Carb Cap
Restricts airflow to create lower pressure, reducing effective boiling point for lower-temperature extraction.

### Variable Power Supply Unit (VPSU)
Consumer equivalent: Battery / Mod / 510-Thread
Adjustable voltage/wattage power delivery for 510-thread cartridges. Range: 1.8V–4.2V.

### Internal Dispersion Tube
Consumer equivalent: Downstem
Submerged tube dispersing aerosol into water through diffusion slits. Multiple lengths and joint sizes.

### Clinical Extract Applicator
Consumer equivalent: Dab Tool
Precision instrument for measuring, handling, and applying concentrate doses.

### Atmospherically-Stabilized Storage Vessel
Consumer equivalent: Storage Jar
Airtight container maintaining optimal humidity and preventing oxidation.

### Borosilicate Glass Respiratory Aid
Consumer equivalent: Glass Pipe / Spoon
Borosilicate glass device for direct thermal extraction without water filtration.

### Chemical Sterilization Agent
Consumer equivalent: Cleaning Solution
Specialized cleaning solutions for maintaining sterile airpath conditions.

### Material Receptacle
Consumer equivalent: Bowl
Chamber holding botanical material during extraction. Multiple joint sizes and materials.

## Materials Science Reference

### Borosilicate Glass
Chemical: SiO₂ + B₂O₃, COE 33
Thermal shock resistant to 515°F. Chemically inert. No off-gassing below melting point (1,648°F). Laboratory and medical device standard.

### Surgical-Grade Stainless Steel (316L)
Chemical: Fe-Cr-Ni-Mo, low carbon <0.03%
16-18% chromium, 10-14% nickel, 2-3% molybdenum. Used in surgical implants and food processing.

### Lab-Grade Quartz
Chemical: SiO₂ >99.9% purity
Melting point 3,110°F. Completely inert at vaporization temps. Preferred for concentrate surfaces.

### Synthetic Sapphire
Chemical: Al₂O₃ aluminum oxide
Mohs hardness 9. Zero off-gassing. Used in Dr. Dabber atomizers. Purest concentrate surface available.

### Zirconia Ceramic
Chemical: ZrO₂ zirconium dioxide
Bioinert, used in dental implants. Natural thermal insulation. Used in DaVinci airpaths.

### PEEK Thermoplastic
Chemical: Polyether ether ketone
Service temp 480°F. No off-gassing below 570°F. FDA food-contact compliant. Used in Storz & Bickel housings.

### Platinum-Cured Silicone
Chemical: PDMS polydimethylsiloxane
FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 compliant. Stable -76°F to 500°F. Used in Softglass. Dishwasher/freezer safe.

### Grade 2 Titanium
Chemical: Ti commercially pure
Biocompatible. Used in orthopedic implants. Does not corrode. Used in DynaVap tips.

### Anodized Aluminum 6061-T6
Chemical: Al alloy with oxide coating
Anodized layer is aluminum oxide (chemically identical to sapphire). Prevents direct aluminum contact.

### Alumina Ceramic
Chemical: Al₂O₃ polycrystalline
Same chemistry as sapphire, polycrystalline form. Bioinert. Common in portable vaporizer ovens.

## Temperature Science

### Key Temperature Thresholds
- 325°F (163°C): THC begins to vaporize. Light vapor.
- 338°F (170°C): CBD begins to vaporize.
- 356°F (180°C): Optimal flavor zone. Terpenes fully expressed.
- 374°F (190°C): Full-spectrum extraction. Dense aerosol.
- 392°F (200°C): Maximum potency zone.
- 410°F (210°C): High-intensity extraction. Some flavor degradation.
- 455°F (235°C): COMBUSTION THRESHOLD. No approved device operates above this.

### Recommendations by Level
- New users: Start 356°F, increase 5°F per session
- Flavor-focused: 356°F–374°F
- Balanced: 374°F–392°F
- Potency-focused: 392°F–410°F
- Concentrates: 450°F–600°F in dedicated devices only

## Compatibility Reference

### Glass Joint Sizes
- 10mm: Small accessories, concentrate rigs, DynaVap water adapters
- 14mm: Most common. Standard for mid-size water pipes and rigs
- 18mm: Large water pipes, desktop setups, high-flow applications
- Gender: Male joints insert INTO female joints. Buy opposite gender of your pipe's joint.

### 510-Thread Compatibility
- All Cartisan and Rokin batteries use standard 510 threading
- Any 510-thread cartridge works with any 510-thread battery
- Thick oils: 3.4V+. Thin oils: 2.0V–3.0V

### Puffco Ecosystem
- Peak Pro atomizers (3D, 3DXL) fit Peak Pro only (not original Peak)
- Peak glass tops: original and Pro NOT interchangeable
- Hot Knife works with both Peak and Peak Pro
- Proxy glass is Proxy-specific only

### Storz & Bickel Ecosystem
- Volcano EASY VALVE bags cross-compatible across Classic, Digit, Hybrid
- Mighty/Mighty+ share cooling units. Crafty/Crafty+ share cooling units. NOT interchangeable between models.
- Dosing capsules universal across Mighty, Crafty, Plenty, Volcano (with adapter)

### Softglass Modular System
- ALL Softglass pieces use same snap-lock connection
- Any base connects to any top. Percolator inserts interchangeable.
- Standard 14mm glass accessories compatible

Q: What is the combustion threshold?
A: 455°F (235°C). Above this, material combusts instead of vaporizing. All registry devices operate below this.

Q: What is an isolated airpath?
A: Vapor contacts only inert materials (glass, ceramic, stainless steel, quartz, sapphire) — no plastic, adhesive, or paint. Gold standard for purity. Read: pixies-pantry.com/knowledge/isolated-airpath/

Q: Desktop vs portable?
A: Desktop: superior precision, larger chambers, no battery limits (Volcano Hybrid, Plenty). Portable: convenience, mobility (Mighty+, PAX). See pixies-pantry.com/compare-desktop-devices/

Q: Convection vs conduction?
A: Conduction: material touches heated surface (faster, can be uneven). Convection: hot air passes through (slower, more even, better flavor). Hybrid: both (Mighty+, Crafty+).

Q: Is silicone safe?
A: Platinum-cured silicone (Softglass, Eyce) is FDA food-contact compliant, does not off-gas. We only carry platinum-cured. Read: pixies-pantry.com/knowledge/borosilicate-vs-soft-glass/
